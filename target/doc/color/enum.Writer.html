The io.Writer interface.

Variants map onto the concrete writers color uses:

RustGo
Writer::Discardio.Discard
Writer::Stdoutcolorable.NewColorableStdout()
Writer::Stderrcolorable.NewColorableStderr()
Writer::Buffer*bytes.Buffer/*strings.Builder
Writer::Customany user-supplied io.Writer
